ABOUT THE BOOK

Atomic Habits

"Atomic Habits" by James Clear is a practical guide on how tiny, daily changes compound into massive life transformations.

The book teaches that getting 1% better each day leads to remarkable results over time, focusing on building better systems instead of just setting goals.

✓ Core Concepts & Principles

  • Compounding gains - Getting 1% better each day leads to a 37-fold improvement in a year.
  • Systems over goals - You do not rise to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your systems.
  • Identity-based habits - Focus on who you wish to become rather than just what you want to achieve.
  • FunctioThe Habit Loop - Every habit proceeds through four stages: cue, craving, response, and reward.
